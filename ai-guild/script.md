# AI Guild — Advertisement Script
**Project:** גילדת AI — Professional AI Guild
**Format:** Digital video (social / web)
**Duration:** ~30 seconds (6 scenes × ~5 seconds each)
**Tone:** Cinematic, mysterious, punk-adjacent
**Date:** 2026-03-10

---

## Creative Concept: "The Ones Who Heard It First"

**Core Insight:**
There's a moment before every wave breaks when a small group of people
already feel the water pulling at their feet. AI isn't coming — it landed.
Most professionals are still reading the weather report. Guild members
stopped reading it months ago. They're already in the water.

**The Unexpected Angle:**
Instead of selling *AI knowledge*, we sell the feeling of being ahead of
the translation curve — before everyone else needs a translator. The
metaphor isn't a classroom, a rocket ship, or a brain. It's a *signal* —
the kind some people hear before it becomes noise.

**Visual Language:**
Desaturated palette with single-color spills (amber, electric blue).
Close-up textures. Slow, deliberate camera movement. Feels like a
Scandinavian crime drama made by someone who also loves record stores.

## Production Notes

**No music throughout** — silence is part of the brand. The absence of
a soundtrack makes the scenes feel like you've stepped into something
real, not produced.

**No lip-sync** — all dialogue is voiceover only.

**No cuts within scenes** — each scene is a single unbroken take.
The discomfort of holding on an image is intentional.

**Voiceover casting direction:** Low, unhurried. Not authoritative —
conspiratorial. Like someone who knows something and is choosing to
tell you.

**Color grade reference:** Scene 1–5 desaturated (low saturation,
high contrast). Scene 6 snaps to pure black with single electric blue
accent — the first real color in the film.
