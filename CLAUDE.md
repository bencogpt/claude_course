## Script Generation

Use the `/script` skill to generate a full production-ready advertisement script from a concept description.

### Script Rules (6 scenes, ~30 seconds total)

- Each scene is **up to 5 seconds** of real video footage
- **No cuts within a scene** — each scene is one unbroken take
- **No lip-sync** — all spoken content is voiceover only
- **No music** — silence is a deliberate production choice
- **No text-only slides** — every scene must contain a real filmed image
- Brand/product appears **only in the final scene**

### Scene Tag Format

Every scene must use all four tags:

```
<image>Opening frame: lighting, composition, subject, texture.</image>
<video>Camera movement and subject action. No cuts.</video>
<voiceover>One or two sentences maximum.</voiceover>
<editing>Transitions, on-screen text, color/pacing notes.</editing>
```

### Quality Bar

Before saving a script, verify:
- The opening image is unexpected — not a screen, not a logo, not a skyline
- At least one scene has no voiceover (silence speaks)
- No scene description uses: "innovative," "future," "AI-powered," "cutting-edge," "revolutionary"
- The voiceover lines could not appear in any other advertisement
